Privacy Policy

This page covers the personal applications published under this site, including the Google OAuth client named rclone, which is used by its owner to synchronise their own files with their own Google Drive account using rclone.

Who runs this

These are personal, non-commercial projects run by a single individual. There is no company, no team, and no third party involved.

What data is accessed

The rclone OAuth client requests access to the Google Drive account of the person who signs in. It is used only by its owner, on their own machine, against their own account. Google Drive file contents and metadata are read and written by the rclone program running locally.

What data is collected or stored

None. No data is collected, transmitted to, or stored on any server operated by this site or its owner. The OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored only in the local rclone configuration file on the owner's own computer.

What data is shared

Nothing is shared with anyone. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party processors, no data sales.

Retention and deletion

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through GitHub at github.com/wimcraft.
